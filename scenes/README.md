# Sample scenes and HDRIs

Everything here is generated by the scripts under `scripts/`; the geometry and the pictures are embedded, so
each glTF is self-contained. Render with `render-cli` (`--mode stylized` for the Stylization pass) or open in
the viewer: `./target/release/viewer scenes/NAME.gltf --hdri assets/hdri/sunny.hdr`.

| Scene | What it shows | Regenerate |
|---|---|---|
| `showcase.gltf` | one sphere per M2 material feature (clearcoat, sheen, dispersion, toon, rough metal) | `gen_showcase_scene.py OUT` |
| `sheen_compare.gltf` | sheen roughness and color against no sheen | `... OUT sheen` |
| `matcap_compare.gltf` | physical, clay, metal and toon MatCaps, and a half-strength blend | `... OUT matcap` |
| `toon_compare.gltf` | five Toon recipes: two hard bands, four soft bands, rim light, highlight ramp, shadow palette with a thick outline | `... OUT toon` |
| `lights_compare.gltf` | the same Toon sphere under five colored lights listed weakest first (light slots follow strength) | `... OUT lights` |
| `glass_compare.gltf` | transmission at roughness 0, 0.15, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8 (checks the multiple-scattering compensation; try `--no-multiscatter`) | `... OUT glass` |

`assets/hdri/` holds small procedural environment maps from `scripts/gen_hdri.py assets/hdri`: `sunny` (sun and
sky), `sunset` (low warm sun), `overcast` (sky gradient, no sun), `softbox` (one broad soft light) and `uniform`
(a white furnace). They are original, so they carry no third-party license.
